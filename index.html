Ancillary & In-situ Constants
Amenity & Open Space (UDCPR)
Amenity space: nil below 20,000 m², 5% at/above. Recreational open space: 10% once plot exceeds 4,000 m² — independent provisions, both deducted before Basic FSI.
Inclusive Housing (MHADA) — Reg. 3.8
Mandatory in Municipal Corporations (pop. > 10 lakh) for residential plots ≥ 4,000 m²: 20% of plot area or Basic FSI for EWS/LIG via MHADA. Equivalent built-up is granted back as incentive.
